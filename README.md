# Privacy Policy for WORD POP!

Last updated: October 31, 2026

## Overview
WORD POP! is a free mobile word puzzle game developed by diggyfiggy studios. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

## Data We Collect
The game itself does not collect personal information. However, we use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect:

- Device advertising ID
- IP address
- Device type and operating system
- Approximate location (derived from IP)
- Ad interaction data

This data is used by Google to serve and measure ads.

## How We Use Data
- To display advertisements
- To improve ad performance and relevance
- No data is sold to third parties by us

## Third-Party Services
- **Google AdMob** – for advertising.  
  See Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Storage
The game stores your high score and mute setting locally on your device only. This data is not sent to any server.

## Children’s Privacy
The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes
We may update this policy. Continued use of the game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
